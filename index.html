OUR BIO

Built around the hobby and its community.

Commonwealth Cards was built on a simple idea: create the kind of card shop Brad would want to spend time in. Whether your passion is Sports cards, Pokémon, Disney, Star Wars, Magic: The Gathering, or other trading card games, we want Commonwealth Cards to be a place where everyone feels welcome.

Brad has called this area home for nearly 20 years while building a successful government contracting business. His deep roots in the community, combined with his lifelong love of collecting, are what brought Commonwealth Cards to life.

Our vision is simple: a shop built on honesty, knowledge, and genuine relationships. We believe every collector deserves to be treated with respect—whether you're buying your first pack, chasing a grail card, submitting cards for grading, trading with friends, or adding the next centerpiece to your collection.

Our goal is to create more than just a retail store. We want Commonwealth Cards to become a community hub where collectors can gather, learn, trade, rip packs, attend events, and share their passion with others. From weekly trade nights and family-friendly events to helping collectors complete sets or find that one elusive card, we're committed to making every visit worthwhile.

Thank you for being part of our journey. We can't wait to welcome you into the Commonwealth Cards community.
